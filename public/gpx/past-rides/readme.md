## Edmond Kingfisher Guthrie Loop — Sunday, August 23, 2026

**125.7 mi, ~3.0 hrs riding time**

A loop out of Edmond heading west to Kingfisher, then north through the Hennessey
area before turning east and coming back south through **Guthrie** — Oklahoma's
original territorial capital (1890–1910), still known for its well-preserved
collection of late-19th-century red-brick storefronts, one of the largest
intact historic downtown districts in the country. From Guthrie the route
headed south back home to Edmond.
