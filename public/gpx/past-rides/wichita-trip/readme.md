# Wichita Mountains Trip

A 2-day round trip out of Edmond, OK to the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge in
southwestern Oklahoma.

## Day 1 — Saturday, October 10, 2026

**402.9 mi, ~8.8 hrs riding time, 8:20 AM – 8:40 PM**

Left Edmond heading west on the old Route 66/I-40 corridor, through El Reno, Hinton,
Weatherford, Clinton, and Elk City, continuing on to Sayre and Erick near the Texas
line. From there the route turned into the **Sandy Sanders Wildlife Management Area**
for a stretch of off-road trail riding — a large public OHV/hunting area in Roger
Mills County known for its network of dirt trails through sand sage and mixed-grass
prairie. After working through the trails, the route headed back east across
southwestern Oklahoma through Altus, Blair, and Snyder, ending the day in **Lawton**.

## Day 2 — Sunday, October 11, 2026

**226.0 mi, ~4.6 hrs riding time, 7:56 AM – 1:00 PM**

A shorter morning loop through the heart of the **Wichita Mountains Wildlife
Refuge** — past Mount Scott and the Meers area (home of the well-known Meers Store,
famous for its longhorn burgers) and Mountain Park on the refuge's west side. From
there the route swung east past Lawton/Fort Sill and Duncan before heading straight
north back home to Edmond.
